Parts of a Letter

Read the key idea, review the examples, and remember the practical tips.

THE BIG IDEA

A formal letter follows a familiar structure so the reader can identify the sender, purpose, message, and next step. The exact format can vary by institution or workplace.

Explanation

The usual parts are the sender’s address or letterhead, date, recipient’s address, salutation, body, complimentary close, signature, and sometimes an enclosure or reference line. Keep the tone respectful and the purpose clear.

Important concepts

  • Heading/letterhead: identifies the sender.
  • Date: shows when the letter was written.
  • Inside address: identifies the recipient.
  • Salutation: greets the recipient.
  • Body: states the purpose and supporting details.
  • Closing and signature: end the letter professionally.

SAMPLE FORMAL LETTER

Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro October 11, 2026

Ms. Elam Jane L. Mitante
Subject Teacher City College of Calapan Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro

Dear Ms. Mitante,

I hope this letter finds you well. I am writing to respectfully request a copy of the updated laboratory schedule for the current semester. Having access to the latest schedule would help me plan my academic activities accordingly and ensure that I am aware of the designated laboratory sessions and any changes to the existing timetable.

If possible, I would greatly appreciate receiving a copy of the updated schedule by Friday so that I can make the necessary preparations in advance. If there are any requirements or procedures I need to follow to obtain the document, please let me know at your convenience.

I understand that you may have other responsibilities and commitments, and I sincerely appreciate the time and effort you dedicate to assisting students with their academic needs. Your assistance would be very helpful in ensuring that I remain informed and well-prepared for my laboratory classes.

Thank you very much for your time, consideration, and assistance regarding this request. I look forward to your response and would be grateful for any information you could provide.

Sincerely,


Wendy-Ann Chitongco
BSIS 3rd Year Student

Remember this ✿Use a subject line when the format allows it, especially for business correspondence. Proofread names, dates, titles, and contact details before sending.
